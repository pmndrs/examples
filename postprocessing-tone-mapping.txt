  - BROKEN (July 2026, fix deferred): the demo renders, but the SSR pass's MRTMaterial logs a THREE.WebGLProgram shader-compile error every frame ("vViewPosition: undeclared identifier"), so the reflections are missing.
  - Cause: three r152 renamed the TANGENTSPACE_NORMALMAP shader define to USE_NORMALMAP_TANGENTSPACE. The vendored screen-space-reflections MRTMaterial (bundled inside @react-three/postprocessing as the SSR effect) declares and assigns the vViewPosition varying only under the old define, while three r165's normal_fragment_begin calls getTangentFrame(-vViewPosition, ...) under the new one. The material also lacks the normalMapTransform uniform that r165's uv chunk uses to compute vNormalMapUv, which degenerates normal-map sampling even once the define is aligned.
  - No released package version fixes this: newer @react-three/postprocessing removed the SSR effect entirely, and 2.16.2 (the declared range) fails identically against three r165.
  - Known fixes when ready: a pnpm patch, verified working, that adds the renamed define to the three conditions gating vViewPosition and supplies a Matrix3 normalMapTransform uniform; or migrate the demo to realism-effects.
